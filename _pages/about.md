I am a Doctoral Researcher at the Centre for Wireless Communications (CWC) – Radio Technologies, University of Oulu, Finland, where I started in January 2025. My main supervisor is [Prof. Markku Juntti](https://www.oulu.fi/en/researchers/markku-juntti), and my co-supervisor is [Prof. Nhan Nguyen](https://nhanng9115.github.io/homepage/). I am also a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Fellow in the [EXACT-6G](https://www.exact6g.eu/) project, funded by the MSCA Doctoral Network program.

Prior to joining the University of Oulu, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) under the guidance of [Prof. Zehui Xiong](https://sites.google.com/view/zehuixiong). I obtained my Master’s degree from Chung-Ang University (CAU), South Korea, in 2024, under the supervision of [Prof. Sungrae Cho](https://sites.google.com/uclab.re.kr/srcho), with close collaboration from [Prof. Nhu-Ngoc Dao](https://pshhlab.github.io/). I received my Bachelor’s degree from the Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), Vietnam, in 2020.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and wireless communications. I focus on developing scalable and low-complexity solutions for advanced wireless systems, including cell-free massive MIMO and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, using techniques such as deep reinforcement learning and deep unfolding.
